How we fill a role

From your brief to their first day.

Six steps, one owner. You see a short list of people who fit, not a pile of CVs.

  1. 01Your requirement

    You tell us the role, the skills, the location and when you need them.

  2. 02Sourcing

    Our recruiters search their networks across six offices in Asia Pacific.

  3. 03Screening

    Each CV is checked against your brief before it goes anywhere.

  4. 04Shortlist to you

    A delivery lead signs off every profile we send you.

  5. 05Interview

    You meet the people. We handle the scheduling and the feedback.

  6. 06On board

    Your new team member starts, and we stay in touch after day one.
