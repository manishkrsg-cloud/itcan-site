This notice explains how ITCAN Pte Ltd (UEN 200413557M) handles personal data in connection with www.itcan.biz (the Site), in line with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. What we collect

  • Messages you send us. The contact form and the call-back form on the Site do not send anything to our servers. They open your own email app with a drafted message. We receive your name, email address, company, phone number and message only if you choose to send that email.
  • Job applications. CVs and enquiries you email to jobs@itcan.biz or hr@itcan.biz.
  • No tracking. The Site does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

2. Settings stored on your device

Two small settings stay in your browser’s storage, never on our servers:

  • itcan-theme (local storage) remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme.
  • itcan-intro (session storage) remembers that you have seen the opening animation in this visit.

You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

3. Embedded videos

Videos load from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you press play. From that point, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

4. How we use personal data

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare proposals.
  • To assess job applications and match candidates with suitable roles.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including those of a licensed employment agency.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Who we share it with

  • ITCAN offices in Singapore, Malaysia, Australia, Hong Kong, India and Indonesia, where needed to handle your enquiry.
  • Service providers that support our operations, such as email hosting, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Clients, when you apply for a role, and only with your consent.

When personal data leaves Singapore, we take steps to make sure it receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. We use reasonable security arrangements to protect it, and the Site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw your consent to its use, by writing to our Data Protection Officer at info@itcan.biz. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
